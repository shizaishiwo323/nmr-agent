# Zou2016LCurveSlope

- **Citation (AGU)**: Zou, Y.-L., Xie, R.-H., & Arad, A. (2016). Numerical estimation of choice of the regularization parameter for NMR T2 inversion. Petroleum Science, 13(2), 237–246. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12182-016-0093-6
- **In-text citation**: (Zou et al., 2016)
- **Suggested citation format**: `[@Zou2016LCurveSlope]`

## Conclusions usable by the agent (checked against the original)

- The regularization parameter can be chosen from the slope of the L-curve: the selected point is where the reciprocal of the absolute slope reaches a threshold in the range 0.1–10, after smoothing of the norms (Section 3.5). The default L-curve inversion of this tool uses this criterion with a threshold of 0.25.
